All fonts bundled in this directory are distributed under the
**SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1** (OFL-1.1), which permits bundling,
redistribution, and embedding in documents. The kit's MIT license does **not**
apply to these files.

| Family | Files | Copyright |
|---|---|---|
| Inter | `Inter-*.ttf` | © The Inter Project Authors (Rasmus Andersson) — <https://github.com/rsms/inter> |
| JetBrains Mono | `JetBrainsMono-*.ttf` | © 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors — <https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono> |
| Newsreader | `Newsreader-*.ttf` | © 2020 The Newsreader Project Authors (Production Type) — <https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader> |

The files are static instances of the families' variable masters in
[google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts), pinned to one commit and
rebuilt by `scripts/fonts.sh` (Inter at optical size 14, Newsreader at 16:
the sizes felipefontoura.com renders). The full OFL-1.1 text of each family
is next to them (`OFL-inter.txt`, `OFL-newsreader.txt`,
`OFL-jetbrainsmono.txt`). Under the OFL, the fonts may be used, studied,
modified, and redistributed freely as long as they are not sold by themselves
and derivative fonts do not reuse the Reserved Font Names.
